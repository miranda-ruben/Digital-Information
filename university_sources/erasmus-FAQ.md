# Erasmus+ Mobility contract (Grant/mobility agreement) - FAQ

## What is a mobility contract?
The mobility contract is a compulsory administrative document which contains all the information concerning your semester of mobility. If you are eligible for a grant, it also mentions the expected amount of the grant and the obligations to be fulfilled in order to obtain its payment. It must be signed by you and by the Vice-Rector before your departure or during the 1st month of mobility. The Outgoing Mobility Office will send you back the version countersigned by the Vice-Rector.

## What should I do if my personal information is not correct?
If your personal information is not correct, please report it immediately to bri.outgoing@uni.lu. NB: If the address on your mobility contract is wrong and/or different from the one given on the guichet etudiant, this does not require any modification of your mobility contract. However, the address given on the guichet etudiant must be the correct one, if it is not the case, please proceed with the modifications.

## What should I do if my bank details are wrong? (for students with an Erasmus+ grant)
The bank account on your contract is the one on which you will receive your Erasmus+ grant. If it is incorrect, please report it immediately to bri.outgoing@uni.lu. We will send you back the corrected contract for signature.

## What is the minimum length of stay?
The minimum length of stay for a mobile student must be at least 60 days, or one semester or one full term at the host university in order to get your mobility validated.

## How will you know the actual dates of my mobility?
During your stay, you will send us certificates signed by your host institution with your arrival and departure dates (see checklist for detailed procedure).

## How will you calculate the exact amount of my grant? (for students with an Erasmus+ grant)
We will recalculate it according to the actual dates of stay indicated on your certificates. If the actual duration of stay is longer than the planned duration, there is no additional scholarship. If it is less, the actual total amount of your scholarship will be reduced.

## When will I receive my Erasmus+ grant? (for students with an Erasmus+ grant)
70%, minimum 4 weeks after receipt of your mobility contract with your signature via the mobility portal. The balance, at least 4 weeks after receipt of the compulsory end-of-stay documents via the mobility portal.

## Can the Erasmus+ grant be combined with the AideFi (formerly Cedies) grant?
Yes, please contact the Financial Aid Service (AideFi) of the Ministry of Higher Education and Research directly for more information.

## Do I need to keep a copy of the mobility contract?
Yes, you must.

## When will I receive the countersigned version of my contract from the Vice-Rector?
Once we have checked that the contract you have submitted meets the requirements.

## I have a question that is not in this FAQ?
Write to us at: bri.outgoing@uni.lu via your Owa student mailbox.

## Key facts
- Steps: Sign the mobility contract before departure or within the 1st month of mobility; upload it via the mobility portal; BRI checks it and returns the version countersigned by the Vice-Rector; keep a copy.
- Form needed: Erasmus+ mobility contract (grant agreement).
- Office responsible: BRI Outgoing (Outgoing Mobility Office), bri.outgoing@uni.lu, from the Owa student mailbox.
- Deadline: Not published as a calendar date. Contract: before departure or during the 1st month of mobility. Grant: 70% at least 4 weeks after the signed contract is received; balance at least 4 weeks after the end-of-stay documents are received. Minimum stay: 60 days or one full semester/term.
