# Attestations Erasmus Studies 2024-25

## Page 1 - Certificate of arrival
(To be signed by the host institution upon your arrival and uploaded in PDF format via the Erasmus+ mobility portal.)
Fields: name and address of the host institution; "We hereby confirm that Mrs / Mr ... enrolled in (study field) ... coming from University of Luxembourg has arrived at our institution"; date of the beginning of the exchange period (including any language course, welcome period); name and function of the authorised person; place; date; signature; stamp.

## Page 2 - Certificate of departure
"This is to certify that ... (name of student), coming from University of Luxembourg, has been enrolled as an ERASMUS student at ... (name of host university)."
Physical mobility period: from ... to ...
Virtual mobility period (if applicable): from ... to ...
Name and function of the authorised person at the host institution; place; date (the date of signature cannot precede the end date, i.e. the date of the last exam); signature; stamp.
(To be signed by the host institution before your return and uploaded in PDF format via the Erasmus+ Mobility Portal.)

BRI Outgoing - Campus Belval - 2, place de l'Universite, L-4365 Esch-sur-Alzette - bri.outgoing@uni.lu

## Key facts
- Steps: Arrival certificate signed by the host on arrival; departure certificate signed before your return; both uploaded in PDF via the Erasmus+ mobility portal.
- Form needed: Certificate of arrival (page 1) and certificate of departure (page 2).
- Office responsible: BRI Outgoing, bri.outgoing@uni.lu.
- Deadline: Not published as a date. Departure certificate: date of signature cannot precede the last exam date.
