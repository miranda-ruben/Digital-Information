# Outgoing student mobility - Exemption

## Overview
Exemptions from the mobility requirement may be granted in certain cases, which "may be subject to change." They are not automatic. Students must request them "as soon as possible" from their faculty's mobility contact, with supporting documents as needed.

## Legal basis
Under Article 27(1) of the Study Regulations, the Rector may grant an exemption on the advice of the Study Programme Director.

## Eligibility criteria
1. Holds a diploma from a recognised foreign higher education institution showing at least four semesters of study.
2. Has at least 30 ECTS credits (or equivalent) from a recognised foreign institution, validated under Article 33(2) of the Act.
3. Has a medical condition requiring specific treatment, or a disability that significantly limits mobility.
4. Has family obligations that prevent leaving home for an extended period.
5. Is a third-country national with a secondary school leaving certificate issued in a third country.

## Supporting documents
- A copy of the foreign higher education diploma.
- A transcript showing at least 30 ECTS, recognised in advance by the Study Programme Director through the "recognition of prior learning/experience" procedure.
- A medical certificate stating that special treatment is required and/or that a disability prevents completing a mobility period.
- A document on your family situation, such as a household composition certificate.
- A secondary school certificate obtained outside the European Economic Area.
