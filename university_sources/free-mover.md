# Outgoing student mobility - Free mover

## Status
- Free-mover mobilities for outgoing students are no longer offered. Requests are not processed from academic year 2026/27. The free-mover option was available only until 2025/26.
- The page describes the free-mover option as a semester abroad at a non-partner institution, where the student organizes the mobility independently with the Outgoing Mobility Office's support.

## Semester of departure
- Bachelor: usually semester 3, 4 or 5.
- Master: semester 3, to be verified against your studies.

## Steps (as described for the free mover option up to 2025/26)
1. Contact the host institution early. Ask its International Relations Department whether it accepts students outside an existing agreement. Check application procedures, deadlines, course programme and enrolment fee. Consider course availability, language of instruction, academic calendar and language skills.
2. Notify your faculty contact by email of your departure as a free mover. Copy bri.outgoing@uni.lu. Deadlines: summer semester departure, "from 1 to 31 October"; winter semester departure, "from 1 to 30 April."
3. Apply to the host institution yourself. Deadlines vary by university.
4. Complete the Learning Agreement (LA). The Outgoing Mobility Office issues it after your notification.
   - Minimum of 30 ECTS.
   - Must be signed by your course director.
   - Submit it to Stephanie Anderson by email at stephanie.anderson@uni.lu, completed digitally.
   - Allow at least one week for signature.
   - Modifications are possible in the month after arrival, using the second page, and need approval from the host institution and your course director.
5. Submit the signed LA to the host university following the Outgoing Mobility Office's instructions. Send a copy of the final signed LA to stephanie.anderson@uni.lu.

## Learning Agreement rules for the Faculty of Law, Economics and Finance (FDEF)
- No courses already taken at the University of Luxembourg, and none taught there after your return.
- At most one language course, capped at 5 ECTS. Language courses cannot be in your native language, and cannot exceed B2 level.
- French language courses are not allowed if your studies at the University of Luxembourg are in French.
- Courses must match your field and level. Bachelor courses are not allowed for Master students.
- List course names as they appear in the host curriculum, without translation.
- Once an approved course's exam has taken place, you cannot change it.
- Extra ECTS beyond 30 do not count toward the requirement if another course is failed.
- Course descriptions and workload or contact hours may be requested.

## Credit conversion (non-European institutions, approximate)
- American: 1 credit = 2.5 ECTS
- Canadian, Chinese, Korean, Japanese: 1 credit = 2 ECTS
- Hong Kong, Russian: 1 credit = 1 ECTS
- Australian (University of Newcastle): 1 unit = 0.75 ECTS
- Israeli: 1 credit = 1.5 ECTS
- Other countries: contact Stephanie Anderson.

## Modifying the LA
- Changes may be needed due to unavailable courses, schedule overlaps or a change of mind. Send the modified LA as soon as possible, and before the host's modification deadline.
- The modification must list kept, deleted and added courses. Total ECTS must still reach 30.
- If your initial LA is approved and unchanged, no new submission is needed.

## Before departure
- Enrol at the University of Luxembourg as an outgoing student ("international exchanges") and at the host institution.
- Pay registration fees only to the host institution.
- Ensure adequate health insurance.
- Sign the mobility contract with the University of Luxembourg (the page refers to a "Mobility F.A.Q (PDF)").

## Transcript and credit validation
- Request the host university to send the transcript directly to Stephanie Anderson by email or post. Printouts of email attachments are not accepted.
- Postal address: Universite du Luxembourg, Faculte de Droit, d'Economie et de Finance, Attn: Stephanie Anderson, 2, avenue de l'universite, 4365 Esch-sur-Alzette, Luxembourg.
- Only credits from courses approved on the final LA are validated. Grades are not converted.

## Failed exams
- Ask the host university about re-sits. A second transcript will be considered.
- If re-sits are not possible, consult "Dettes de mobilite" in your study programme's Moodle documentation (usually the "Regles de fonctionnement..." document), or contact your study programme administrator.

## Accommodation
Students must find their own accommodation. The host institution's website may help. Apply early, since exchange rooms are limited.

## Mobility Fund
Bachelor students who go as free movers to an institution outside the Greater Region may be eligible for the University's "Fonds de mobilite." The grant is paid after return, once all requested documents are submitted.

## Communication
The Outgoing Mobility Office contacts students through their student email address (firstname.lastname.index@student.uni.lu).

## Key facts
- Steps: The free mover option is no longer offered: requests are not processed from academic year 2026/27 (it was available only until 2025/26). For earlier years the steps were: contact the host institution; notify the faculty contact and bri.outgoing@uni.lu; apply yourself; complete a learning agreement of at least 30 ECTS; submit the signed LA.
- Form needed: Learning Agreement (issued by the Outgoing Mobility Office after notification).
- Office responsible: Outgoing Mobility Office, bri.outgoing@uni.lu; learning agreements and transcripts: Stephanie Anderson, stephanie.anderson@uni.lu (FDEF).
- Deadline: For the discontinued free mover option: notification 1 to 31 October (summer semester departure) or 1 to 30 April (winter semester departure). No new requests from 2026/27.
