# Outgoing student mobility Erasmus+ - Step 10: After your study exchange ("Welcome back!")

## 1. Transcript of Records
Send your original transcript of records (ToR) to the contact person at your faculty so that your ECTS can be validated. Electronic copies are accepted only if the host university sends them directly to the Outgoing Mobility Office or your faculty's contact person. If you send them yourself, they must be protected or certified.

## 2. ECTS validation
Your faculty validates and integrates the ECTS you earned.

## 3. Diploma Supplement
"Your mobility will appear on the Diploma supplement when you achieve your Bachelor."

## 4. Sharing your experience
- Contact the ESN association (European Student Network) at the University of Luxembourg and join its activities and offers.
- Attend the next Mobility dating event organized by the Outgoing Mobility Office.

## Key facts
- Steps: 1) Send the original transcript of records to your faculty contact (or have the host send it directly). 2) The faculty validates your ECTS. 3) Your mobility appears on the Diploma Supplement at the end of the Bachelor.
- Form needed: None. Original transcript of records from the host university.
- Office responsible: Your faculty's mobility contact (a "Contacts within the faculties" section exists on the live page but its names were not captured; see the Erasmus checklist: FDEF Stephanie Anderson, stephanie.anderson@uni.lu).
- Deadline: Not published on this page.
