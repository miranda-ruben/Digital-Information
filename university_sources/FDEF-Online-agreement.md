# How to create and fill out my Online Learning Agreement (OLA) - FDEF

You have been nominated to go abroad for an exchange. The student must complete the Online Learning Agreement (academic contract - choice of courses) and then get it approved by both responsible coordinators (at your home institution and your host institution). By signing the OLA, they agree to you taking the courses and your home university to validate them upon your return if completed successfully. It is a first provisional version: you will be able to modify it after the start of your mobility in the host institution within their indicated deadline.

## Creating your account and starting
A. Follow the link learning-agreement.eu and create an account to access your Learning Agreement.
B. Log in with "MyAcademicID" (DO NOT use eIDAS or Google).
C. Type "University of Luxembourg" (in English) and select it.
D. Use your Student ID and password to log in.
E. Click on Proceed to register on the MyAcademicID IAM Service.
F. Verify your name, first name and your student e-mail address, confirm the Use policy and submit.
G. You will receive an email to confirm your student e-mail address.
H. Click on the verification link in that email to complete the registration.
I. Complete your personal data, tick the Privacy policy and save.

Important: indicate the right Field of Education (ISCED code) for your study programme, as it is crucial for the review of your learning agreement:
- Bachelor en Droit: Law (0421) (778)
- Bachelor en Gestion: Business and administration (041) (769)
- Bachelor en Sciences economiques: Economics (0311) (759)
- Bachelor Comptabilite et Fiscalite: Accounting and taxation (0411) (770)
- Master en Droit: Law (0421) (778)
- Master in Finance and Science in Economics: Economics (0311) (759)
- Master in Accounting and Audit: Accounting and taxation (0411) (770)
- Master in Entrepreneurship and Innovation: Management and administration (0413) (772)

J. Click on "Create New" to start your Learning Agreement.
K. Select your mobility type: "Semester Mobility".

## Filling in the OLA
1. Student information: your personal data is entered automatically from "My account".
2. Sending institution information: enter the contact information of the Mobility specialist in your Faculty. She will help you establish your Learning Agreement and is in charge of its validation at the University of Luxembourg. Sending Responsible Person: Mobility specialist FDEF, Stephanie Anderson, stephanie.anderson@uni.lu, +352 46 66 44 6187.
3. Receiving institution information: ask your receiving institution for the right contact data (name and email of the responsible person who will sign your OLA, e.g. Erasmus coordinator or course director).
4. Proposed mobility programme - list of courses: enter the planned dates of your mobility semester. Indicate the web link to the course catalogue of your host university (even if not mandatory). Select the main instruction language of your chosen courses and your level (most universities require a minimum B2 level). Click on "Add component to Table A" to list your chosen courses one by one (one component = one course). Per course: course name, course code, number of ECTS, semester (winter/1st or summer/2nd).
   - Choose courses from the correct semester (summer OR winter, depending on your mobility semester).
   - You need to choose courses for at least 30 ECTS in total, otherwise your OLA will not be validated (Bachelor students only). Master students are also advised to choose 30 ECTS.

Faculty requirements from the Mobility specialist / how to choose your courses:
- It might not be possible to choose exactly 30 ECTS (e.g. if all available courses have 7 ECTS, your total must be 35, as enrolling for less than 30 ECTS is not possible).
- You cannot choose extra courses in order to select the ones you wish to have validated after the exams.
- If possible, do not choose courses you have already taken previously nor will take upon your return at Unilux.
- Courses must correspond to your field of study at Unilux (no sports, crafts, etc.).
- If you wish to choose an optional course, indicate it as such (write "optional" behind the title). It will not count towards your required 30 ECTS, even if you fail one of the approved courses. It will be recorded as an optional course and you will graduate with 180+ (bachelor) respectively 120+/60+ (master) ECTS.
- Any ECTS obtained during mobility cannot be used to substitute for previously missed ECTS and will not give you credit for the remaining semesters at Unilux.
- You may choose one language course, but not in your native language, nor French, nor German (for Luxembourgish students that went through the Luxembourgish school system), nor in a language where you have a level higher than B2. Maximum approvable ECTS for a language course are at the discretion of the study programme director.
- List the course names in their original language (as on the host university's curriculum). If the course names are in a language other than French, English, German, Spanish, Portuguese or Italian, provide a translation in French or English in brackets behind the original name.
- At the end of your mobility semester, a Transcript of Records will be provided by the host university. The latest and approved version of your OLA must correspond to the Transcript of Records.

Table B (recognition at the sending institution): enter "Mobility window" in the fields "Component Title" and "Component Code", and "30" in "Number of ECTS credits", then select the semester of departure. You do not have to list your courses taking place at the University of Luxembourg during your absence.

5. Virtual components - Table C: complete this table like Table A ONLY if you will follow some online courses.
6. Commitment - signature and submission: sign with your mouse, then click on "Sign and send ..." to submit your OLA for review. Your OLA is only valid when the status is: "Signed by both coordinators".

Once submitted, the responsible mobility coordinator at Unilux (your sending institution) will have it reviewed by your study programme director and, if approved, will sign it. After approval, the responsible coordinator at the receiving institution is notified to verify and sign it. If changes are required, your OLA will be declined and you will be notified by email. You can follow the status of your OLA in "My Learning Agreements" (also as a PDF). You will also receive status notifications on your uni.lu student email account.

## How to make changes in my Online Learning Agreement
- 1st case: you received a rejection notification by email on your student email account requiring changes: follow the instructions of the email.
- 2nd case: you want to make changes before or during the 1st month of your stay: log in to learning-agreement.eu, go to "My learning agreements" and click "Apply changes". Go to step 2, "Sending Mobility Programme changes and Final LA Table A2", and click "Add Component Final Table A2". You can add or delete components/courses; in both cases complete the field "reason change". Table B should never be changed. Changes of virtual components in Table C are made the same way as for Table A. Sign and submit your modified OLA again for approval; you will be notified when it is signed by both coordinators.

## FAQ
Deadline to change courses at host university: some host universities are very strict with their deadlines to change the course choice and to submit a modified learning agreement. It is absolutely important that you get both universities' (home and host) approval before you make your final course choice. By enrolling in courses without approval you risk not getting them validated even if you successfully complete them.

## Key facts
- Steps: 1) Create an account on learning-agreement.eu with MyAcademicID (University of Luxembourg login). 2) Create a new Learning Agreement, mobility type "Semester Mobility". 3) Enter the FDEF mobility specialist as sending contact and the host contact as receiving contact. 4) List courses in Table A (at least 30 ECTS for Bachelor students) and enter "Mobility window" / 30 ECTS in Table B. 5) Sign and send. 6) The OLA is valid only when signed by both coordinators.
- Form needed: Online Learning Agreement (OLA) at learning-agreement.eu. Changes: "Apply changes" in OLA, or the Word changes form if the host does not use OLA.
- Office responsible: FDEF mobility specialist Stephanie Anderson, stephanie.anderson@uni.lu, +352 46 66 44 6187.
- Deadline: Not published as a date. Changes can be made before or during the 1st month of the stay; the host university may set its own, strict deadlines for course changes.
