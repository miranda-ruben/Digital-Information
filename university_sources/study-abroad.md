Title: Study abroad — Faculty of Law, Economics and Finance (FDEF)
Institution: University of Luxembourg
Official source URL: https://www.uni.lu/fdef-en/study-abroad/
Source last checked: 2026-11-12
Scope: FDEF faculty-specific study abroad guidance. Do not assume FDEF-specific contacts or semester recommendations apply to other faculties.

OVERVIEW
A semester abroad is compulsory for University of Luxembourg bachelor's students. Options described here are Erasmus+ at a European partner university, the Global Exchange Programme (GEP) at a partner university outside Europe, or Free Mover mobility in very specific cases (contact the faculty mobility contact).

CONTACTS
International Relations Office (BRIO): bri.outgoing@uni.lu. Contact BRIO to organise the semester abroad and for administrative matters. An on-site appointment can be made via the Affluences link on the source page.
FDEF faculty mobility contact: Stephanie Anderson, stephanie.anderson@uni.lu, +352 46 66 44 6187. Contact for host university information, host university courses, Learning Agreement approval, recognition of credits earned abroad, or mobility exemption requests.

WHEN TO GO — FDEF PROGRAMME-SPECIFIC
Bachelor in Business Administration: semester 3 or 4 strongly recommended; semester 5 only in exceptional cases such as Global Exchange.
Bachelor en Sciences Economiques and Bachelor en Droit: semester 3, 4 or 5.
Erasmus+ selection takes place at the beginning of the summer semester for the following full academic year (winter or summer mobility). For mobility in semester 3 or 4, apply at the beginning of semester 2. For mobility in semester 5, apply at the beginning of semester 4.
Global Exchange Programme: only possible in semester 5; selection takes place in November after Study Abroad Day for the following academic year's winter semester; apply in semester 3.
The source page does not state a specific calendar-date Erasmus application deadline. Do not invent one; consult the linked application procedure or BRIO.

LEARNING AGREEMENT
The Learning Agreement records provisional courses at the host university. It must first be approved by the University of Luxembourg home study programme and then by the host university. After arrival, course choices may be modified based on availability and schedules. A fully signed final Learning Agreement is required by the end of mobility to validate successfully completed courses.
Erasmus+ partners are transitioning to the Online Learning Agreement (OLA), but not all accept it. The BRIO instruction email provides OLA instructions for Erasmus+ partners or the relevant Word template for Global Exchange. Global Exchange Learning Agreements cannot be completed via OLA.
For full instructions, follow the FDEF Learning Agreement Specifics link on the official source page.

BEFORE, DURING AND AFTER MOBILITY
The source page links to Erasmus+ checklists for before, during and after the stay. Consult those linked checklists for specific actions and deadlines; their full contents are not included in this file.

RECOGNITION OF CREDITS
The host university issues a Transcript of Records (TOR) for recognition of ECTS. The courses on the transcript must match the final approved Learning Agreement. Students are responsible for obtaining the transcript and should ask the host university to send a copy to stephanie.anderson@uni.lu. A transcript submitted directly by a student is only accepted if protected against alteration or if the original paper version is presented.

EXEMPTIONS
Mobility exemptions are possible for bachelor students under specific criteria. Students who think they qualify should email stephanie.anderson@uni.lu; the faculty evaluates the request and confirms approval if applicable. The detailed criteria are on a separate linked page, not reproduced here.
