# Possible departure semesters

Contact for all faculties: International Relations Office - bri.outgoing@uni.lu

## FDEF (Faculty of Law, Economics and Finance)
- Bachelor en Droit: semester 3, 4, 5
- Bachelor en Sciences Economiques: semester 3, 4, 5
- Bachelor en Comptabilite et Fiscalite: semester 4
- Bachelor in Business Administration: semester 3, 4; semester 5 (exceptionally)
- Masters (except Masters en droit): semester 3. (Masters en droit: semester 2.)

## FSTM (Faculty of Science, Technology and Medicine)
- Bachelor in Computer Science: semester 4
- Bachelor in Applied Information Technology: semester 3, 4
- Bachelor en Sciences de la Vie - Filiere Biologie: semester 3, 4, 5
- Bachelor in Nursing: semester 5
- Bachelor in Mathematics: semester 5
- Bachelor in Engineering: semester 4
- Bachelor in Physics: semester 3, 4 (students enrolled in semester 1); semester 5 (students enrolled in semester 3)
- Masters: semester 3

## FHSE (Faculty of Humanities, Education and Social Sciences)
- BCE - Etudes Francaises: semester 5
- BCE - Histoire: semester 3, 4, 5
- BCE - Philosophie: semester 4, 5
- BCE - Germanistik: semester 4, 5
- BCE - Luxemburgistik: semester 5
- BCE - English Studies: semester 5
- Bachelor en Enseignement Musical: semester 4
- Bachelor in Animation: semester 4
- Bachelor in Psychology: semester 3, 4
- Bachelor en Sciences de l'Education: semester 3, 4
- Bachelor en Sciences Sociales et Educatives: semester 4
- Masters: semester 3

## Key facts
- Steps: Find your programme in the list for your faculty to see which semesters you can go abroad.
- Form needed: None in this document.
- Office responsible: International Relations Office (BRI), bri.outgoing@uni.lu.
- Deadline: Not published here. This document only lists the possible semesters; application timing is on the faculty study-abroad page.
