# YOUR EXCHANGE SEMESTER - The world is waiting for you!
All you need to know about your exchange semester.

## Which exchange programme?
- Erasmus+: over 250 partner universities in Europe. Call for Application: March.
- Global Exchange: over 50 partner universities outside Europe. Call for Application: October/November.
- Each programme has an Application Guide (linked in the leaflet).

## Which semester?
See "View departure semester" (the departure-semesters document).

## Financial support
- ERASMUS+ GRANT: contribution to living costs; contribution to traveling costs; Top-Up Inclusion (special cases).
- GLOBAL EXCHANGE GRANT: contribution to living costs.

## Contact
The International Relations Office will help you: bri.outgoing@uni.lu

## Key facts
- Steps: Choose a programme (Erasmus+ in Europe or Global Exchange outside Europe), check the possible departure semester, apply during the call.
- Form needed: Application per programme (see the Application Guide linked in the leaflet).
- Office responsible: International Relations Office, bri.outgoing@uni.lu.
- Deadline: Not published as a date. Erasmus+ call: March. Global Exchange call: October/November (no year given).
