# International Relations Office

## Overview
The office supports university leadership, academic and research staff, and students with international projects, which raises the university's international profile. Its core work is designing, negotiating, preparing and running student mobility, mainly at the Bachelor's level, where it is mandatory.

## Responsibilities
- Coordinates and promotes the Erasmus+ programme, mainly student mobility
- Administers staff mobility
- Manages agreements with international partner universities outside Erasmus+
- Co-manages the Guillaume Dupaix international master's scholarships with the SEVE
- Informs students and staff about international opportunities
- Organises and takes part in international visits

## Key figures
- Over 550 Erasmus+ agreements
- Annual mobility of about 600 outgoing and 250 incoming students
- A managed budget of over 1 million EUR
- Oversight of the Erasmus+ project, including International Credit Mobility (ICM) for students and staff, with a budget of about 500k EUR
- Close to 100 other international agreements

## Other activities
- Global exchange programme: study abroad options beyond Europe
- Top Student Prize: each year, three top students per Faculty are invited to an international summer school abroad
- Ministry-funded project: finances the Guillaume Dupaix master's scholarships and incoming mobility scholarships, with support from the Ministry of Foreign and European Affairs
- Development cooperation projects in Luxembourg's development cooperation target countries
- University of the Greater Region (UniGR)
- EURAXESS: the office hosts Luxembourg's representative for this pan-European initiative for researchers
