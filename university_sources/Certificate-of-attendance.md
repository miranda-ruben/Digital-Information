# Certificate of attendance

Name and address of the host institution (to be filled in).

Left part - CERTIFICATE OF ARRIVAL (to be signed by the host institution upon your arrival and downloaded in PDF format via the Erasmus+ Mobility Portal): "We hereby confirm that Mrs / Mr ... enrolled in (study field) ... coming from University of Luxembourg has arrived at our institution." Start date of the exchange period (including any language course, welcome period). Name and function of the authorised person, place, date, signature, stamp.

Right part - CERTIFICATE OF DEPARTURE (to be signed by the host institution before your return and uploaded with both parts completed, signed and stamped in PDF format via the Erasmus+ Mobility Portal): "We hereby confirm that Mrs / Mr ... has left our institution." End date of the exchange period (last exam date). Name and function of the authorised person, place, date (date of signature cannot precede the end date), signature, stamp.

Certificates of departure with the left part empty cannot be accepted.

BRI Outgoing - Campus Belval - 2, place de l'Universite, L-4365 Esch-sur-Alzette - bri.outgoing@uni.lu

## Key facts
- Steps: Arrival part signed by the host on arrival; departure part signed before your return (date of signature cannot precede the last exam date); upload the complete form (both parts) as PDF via the Erasmus+ Mobility Portal.
- Form needed: This certificate of attendance (arrival and departure).
- Office responsible: BRI Outgoing, bri.outgoing@uni.lu.
- Deadline: Not published as a date. The departure part must be signed on or after the last exam date and before return.
