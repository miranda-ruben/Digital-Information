# MY CHECKLIST - Compulsory steps - ERASMUS+

I regularly check my unilu mailbox @student.uni.lu

## 1. Before leaving
- Is my European health insurance card valid for the whole mobility period?
- I download the App International SOS assistance (this is not a health insurance!). I follow the instructions received via email.
- Luxembourgish students: for safety reason, I report my trip to the MAEE.

## 2. Arriving at the host university
- I make sure my certificate of arrival (1) is completed, signed and stamped by the host university.
- I then upload a PDF copy of the above certificate onto the Erasmus+ mobility portal.
- If I have the Erasmus+ grant, I make sure my bank details indicated on my mobility contract, match the information on my guichet étudiant.

## 3. Within the month of arrival
- If there are any changes in courses or the ECTS: I must update my learning agreement following the instructions provided on Tutorial_Online_Learning_Agreement (2) received by email.

(2) If my host university doesn't use the online form (OLA), I complete the form "during the mobility" of the learning agreement (format Word), I sign it, I have it validated by my host university (min. 30 ECTS), then I send it back for validation:
- FSTM: fstm.studentmobility@uni.lu
- FDEF: Stephanie Anderson, stephanie.anderson@uni.lu
- FHSE: Nathalie Charpentier, nathalie.charpentier@uni.lu

## 4. On leaving the host university
- I make sure the certificate of end of stay (1) is completed, signed and stamped by the host university.
- I upload a PDF copy of the above document via the Erasmus+ mobility portal.
- I upload a PDF copy of my learning agreement and of the modifications (if any) signed by all parties, via Erasmus+ mobility portal.
- I complete my online mobility report (invitation received in my Unilu student inbox) - no document required.
- If I have the Erasmus+ grant AND if 2 to 4 are ticked, I will receive the 2nd payment (the remaining amount will be calculated) within around 4 weeks after submitting the certificate of end of stay.

## 5. Upon returning from mobility
- I provide my original transcript of records from the host university (3) to my academic contact in order to validate my ECTS:
  - FSTM: FSTM students mobility (MSA 6eme et.)
  - FDEF: Stephanie Anderson (MSA 6eme et.)
  - FHSE: Nathalie Charpentier (MSA 6eme et.)

(3) An electronic version will only be accepted if the host university sends it directly to the academic contact. If not, it will need to be a certified and protected copy.

(1) Download documents on https://www.uni.lu/en/mobility/outgoing-student-mobility/erasmus
Keep a copy of all my documents.
Mobility Contact: BRI Outgoing - bri.outgoing@uni.lu

## Key facts
- Steps: 1) Before leaving: valid European health insurance card, International SOS app, MAEE report (Luxembourgish students). 2) On arrival: certificate of arrival signed and stamped, uploaded to the Erasmus+ mobility portal; bank details must match the guichet etudiant. 3) Within the month of arrival: update the learning agreement if courses or ECTS change. 4) On leaving: certificate of end of stay, signed learning agreement and modifications, online mobility report; all uploaded to the portal. 5) On return: original transcript of records to the academic contact.
- Form needed: Certificate of arrival; certificate of end of stay (departure); learning agreement (online OLA, or the Word form "during the mobility" if the host does not use OLA).
- Office responsible: BRI Outgoing, bri.outgoing@uni.lu. Faculty contacts: FDEF Stephanie Anderson (stephanie.anderson@uni.lu); FSTM fstm.studentmobility@uni.lu; FHSE Nathalie Charpentier (nathalie.charpentier@uni.lu).
- Deadline: No calendar dates in this document. Relative timing only: learning agreement updates "within the month of arrival"; second grant payment "within around 4 weeks" after the certificate of end of stay is submitted.
