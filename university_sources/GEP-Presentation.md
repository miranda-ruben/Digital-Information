# Global Exchange Programme in a nutshell
Your exchange semester beyond the borders of Europe.

## Benefits
- Bilateral agreements with many universities worldwide
- No tuition fees at the host university
- Expand your personal network; add new competences to your profile
- Immerse yourself in a new culture; step outside of your comfort zone
- Scholarship opportunities

## Who can apply? Eligibility criteria
- Bachelor or Master student
- For Bachelor students: successfully finished at least one semester at the University of Luxembourg at the time of applying
- A minimum grade of 12 to be able to apply
- Good knowledge of the language of instruction at the partner university
- Approval from your Study Programme Director

## How to apply for academic year 2025/26?
- Choose your university (an updated list of partner universities and available places).
- Choose up to 3 host universities in order of preference.
- A link to the application form is sent by email.

Documents to submit: copy of student card; copy of passport; transcript of records; motivation letter; curriculum vitae.

## Scholarship opportunities
- Mobility fund: ONLY for Bachelor students, 1000 EUR/month.
- Erasmus+ International Credit Mobility: a grant from the European Commission, 700 EUR/month + travel allowance, for: University of Tirana (Albania), International University of Sarajevo (Bosnia and Herzegovina), Ivane Javakhishvili Tbilisi State University (Georgia), North American Private University - International Institute of Technology (Tunisia), Universite Cheikh Anta Diop de Dakar - UCAD (Senegal).

Second call for applications: Spring 2025. Students from these study programmes can participate ONLY in the 2nd call: Bachelor en Sciences Sociales et Educatives; Bachelor en Sciences de l'Education; Bachelor in Computer Science; Bachelor in Applied Information Technology; Bachelor in Physics; Bachelor in Engineering. These apply for the remaining places; online form, exact date to be confirmed beginning of February 2025.

You cannot apply for the Global Exchange and Erasmus+ programme for the same semester. But it is possible to do 2 exchange semesters: first Erasmus+, then GEP.

## Advice
Start your application on time; ask someone to proofread your motivation letter; check if you need a visa; search for accommodation; inform yourself about the local culture; download the International SOS app.
