# Outgoing student mobility Erasmus+ - Step 2: How to participate in the Erasmus+ Programme

## Bachelor students
1. Call (March): a call is sent out in March for the entire following academic year, covering both winter and summer semester mobilities.
2. Online form (early March): eligible students receive an invitation at their student email address to complete an online form through the mobility portal. You can name up to 3 partner universities, ranked by preference, with your first choice listed first. "No changes will be allowed once the form is submitted."
3. Spot allocation: the study programme director allocates Erasmus+ spots based on academic criteria, namely number of credits and average. Choices are not handled first-come, first-served.
4. Acceptance or refusal (end of March / beginning of April): you receive an email at your student address asking you to accept your spot or refuse it (with an explanation), again via the mobility portal.
5. Next steps after you accept your spot: see Step 3 and the following steps (application at the receiving institution).

## Master students
1. Validation: contact the mobility contact person in your faculty to get your mobility project validated by your study programme director.
2. Declare your wish to go (end of March): the Outgoing Mobility Office must receive notice of your wish to go on mobility.
3. Spot allocation: the study programme director allocates the Erasmus+ spots.
4. Acceptance: once spots are allocated, you receive an email at your student address asking you to accept the allocated spot.

## Key facts
- Steps (Bachelor): 1) Call in March. 2) Fill in the online form via the mobility portal (early March), up to 3 universities ranked by preference; no changes after submission. 3) The study programme director allocates spots by number of credits and average. 4) Accept or refuse your spot by email via the mobility portal (end of March / beginning of April).
- Steps (Master): 1) Get your mobility project validated through your faculty mobility contact. 2) Tell the Outgoing Mobility Office of your wish to go (end of March). 3) Spot allocation. 4) Accept the allocated spot by email.
- Form needed: Online application form in the mobility portal (invitation by email).
- Office responsible: Outgoing Mobility Office; study programme director (allocation); faculty mobility contact (Master validation).
- Deadline: Not published as calendar dates. Call in March; online form early March; acceptance or refusal end of March / beginning of April; Master wish to go end of March (no year given on the page).
