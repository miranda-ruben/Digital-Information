# Certificate of arrival

(To be signed by the host institution upon your arrival and uploaded in PDF format via the Erasmus+ mobility portal.)

Form fields: name and address of the host institution; "We hereby confirm that Mrs / Mr ... enrolled in (study field) ... coming from University of Luxembourg has arrived at our institution"; date of the beginning of the exchange period (including any language course, welcome period); name and function of the authorised person at the host institution; place; date; signature; stamp of the institution.

BRI Outgoing - Campus Belval - 2, place de l'Universite, L-4365 Esch-sur-Alzette - bri.outgoing@uni.lu

## Key facts
- Steps: Have the host institution complete, sign and stamp this form on arrival; upload it in PDF format via the Erasmus+ mobility portal.
- Form needed: This certificate of arrival.
- Office responsible: BRI Outgoing, bri.outgoing@uni.lu.
- Deadline: Not published in this document (the checklist describes it as a step on arrival).
