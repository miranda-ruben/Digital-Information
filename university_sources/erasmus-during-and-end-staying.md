# Outgoing student mobility Erasmus+ - Steps 8 and 9

## Step 8: Certificate of Arrival and modification of the LA/OLA if necessary
- Stay in touch with your faculty's mobility contact person, your programme director and the Outgoing Mobility Office during your stay.
- Follow the checklist and upload the required documents via the mobility portal.
- Some courses you chose may not run after you arrive. If so, modify your Learning Agreement (PDF) or Online Learning Agreement (OLA).

## Step 9: Before your return: Certificate of Departure and Mobility Survey
- You will get an email at your student address with the next steps after your exam period.
- Review the Checklist (EN/FR PDF).
- Get the Certificate of Departure signed by your host university and upload it to the portal.
- Get your Learning Agreement and any modification signed by both institutions, and upload them.
- Complete the end-of-stay report (Erasmus+ participant report). It arrives automatically on your mobility contract's end-of-stay date. If you leave early, upload the end-of-stay certificate and submit the form so the dates can be changed and the report invitation sent sooner.
- "All documents and the report are mandatory for the validation of your mobility semester."
- The report invitation comes from eu-corporate-notification-system@ec.europa.eu (EU Corporate Notification System).

## Key facts
- Steps: 8 Upload the certificate of arrival; modify the learning agreement (LA or OLA) if courses change. 9) Before returning: certificate of departure signed by the host and uploaded; learning agreement and modifications signed by both institutions and uploaded; complete the end-of-stay report.
- Form needed: Certificate of arrival; certificate of departure; learning agreement (PDF or OLA) and modifications; Erasmus+ participant (end-of-stay) report.
- Office responsible: Faculty mobility contact, programme director and Outgoing Mobility Office (no contact details on this page; see the checklist).
- Deadline: No fixed dates. The end-of-stay report is tied to the end-of-stay date on the mobility contract; the departure documents are due at the end of the exam period. All are required to validate the mobility semester.
