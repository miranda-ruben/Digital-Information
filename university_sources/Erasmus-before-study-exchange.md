# Erasmus+ - Before your study exchange

## Step 3: Application at the receiving institution
- The Outgoing Mobility Office emails you the application instructions for your host university.
- Winter semester instructions arrive between April and June. Summer semester instructions arrive between late August and November.
- You submit the application to your host university by the host's own deadlines, and you arrange your own housing.
- "Do not start your application before receiving the email from the Outgoing Mobility Office."

## Step 4: Learning Agreement (LA) or Online Learning Agreement (OLA)
- The learning agreement is mandatory to validate your mobility and must cover at least 30 ECTS.
- It must be signed by you, your programme director (through your faculty's mobility contact), and your host institution.
- First check whether your host institution uses a PDF/Word learning agreement or the Online Learning Agreement (OLA).
- Faculty-specific OLA tutorials exist for FSTM, FDEF and FHSE.

## Step 5: Erasmus+ language evaluation / Online Linguistic Support (OLS)
- If the teaching language is not your mother tongue, you take a mandatory language evaluation.
- Results below B2 lead to online language courses.
- This evaluation is usually not accepted as an official language test (such as TOEFL or IELTS).

## Step 6: Erasmus+ grant
- Eligible students receive the grant automatically and do not need to apply.
- Eligibility criteria are in two documents referenced on the page, including "Financial Support for Erasmus student mobility 2023/24" (an older edition; see the 2025-26 funding document for current amounts).

## Step 7: Re-enrolment at the University of Luxembourg and mobility contract
- Enrol as an outgoing "international exchange" student at the University of Luxembourg and as an incoming student at your host university.
- You pay enrolment fees only at the University of Luxembourg, but German universities' "Semesterbeitrag" fees cannot be waived.
- Upload your signed mobility contract through the mobility portal.

## Useful information
- Accommodation is your responsibility. Apply early because rooms are limited. Beware of housing scams, and consider contacting the local ESN association.
- Get adequate health insurance. International SOS provides free 24/7 assistance for mobility trips (not leisure trips), but it is not an insurance provider.
- Check passport validity and visa requirements. Some partners require validity beyond your mobility period.
- The Green Travel top-up covers travel between your main residence and the host university, and back. A "Declaration of Honour Green Travel" is referenced on the page.

## Key facts
- Steps: 3) Wait for the Outgoing Mobility Office email, then apply at the host university. 4) Complete the learning agreement (OLA or PDF/Word), at least 30 ECTS, signed by you, your programme director and the host. 5) Take the language evaluation if the teaching language is not your mother tongue. 6) The Erasmus+ grant is awarded automatically if eligible. 7) Re-enrol at Luxembourg and upload the signed mobility contract through the mobility portal.
- Form needed: Learning Agreement (OLA or PDF/Word); mobility contract (upload through the mobility portal); Declaration of Honour Green Travel (for the Green Travel top-up).
- Office responsible: Outgoing Mobility Office (instructions by email); faculty mobility contacts for the learning agreement.
- Deadline: Not published as calendar dates. Instruction emails: winter semester April to June, summer semester late August to November (verify on the live page). Application deadlines are set by each host university.
