# Outgoing student mobility

## Study abroad
- Studying abroad exposes students to another academic culture, which encourages them to reassess their choices, ways of thinking and approaches to study.
- Open to Bachelor and Master students at the University of Luxembourg.
- The University supports international mobility, following the 1999 Bologna Declaration. The Act of 27 June 2018 on the University's organisation makes it a requirement for Bachelor's degrees: a Bachelor's degree may only be awarded if the student has earned at least 30 ECTS credits at a recognised foreign institution.
- Bachelor students must complete one semester abroad to earn a University of Luxembourg degree.
- The programme director recognises ECTS earned abroad, based on a learning agreement signed beforehand.
- Master students: a semester abroad is optional, though most Master programmes offer it.
- An Erasmus+ traineeship abroad is possible but does not count as compulsory mobility.

## Benefits of academic mobility
Broadens your academic horizon; improves language skills with native speakers; builds independence; fosters an open-minded view of the world; develops an international network; enhances your CV.

## Available programmes
- Erasmus+: exchanges with partner institutions in the European Union.
- Global Exchange Programme: exchanges with partner institutions outside the European Union.
- Free mover: study at a non-partner institution that fits your programme.

## Key facts
- Steps: Choose a programme (Erasmus+, Global Exchange Programme or Free mover); the programme director recognises the ECTS earned abroad based on a learning agreement signed beforehand.
- Form needed: Learning agreement (details on the Erasmus+ step pages).
