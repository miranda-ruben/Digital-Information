# Checklist - outgoing mobility (Free mover / SEMP)

## Before leaving
- Check that your European health insurance card (CNS) is valid for the whole stay abroad.
- Outside Europe, arrange any entry and student visa requirements and take out supplementary health, travel and repatriation insurance.
- Luxembourg students should register their stay with the MAEE for safety reasons (declaration of stay abroad, guichet.public.lu).
- Download the International SOS assistance app and follow the instructions sent by email. It is not a health insurance.

## Within the month of arrival at the host university
- Send the learning agreement, fully completed, signed and stamped by the host university, as a PDF to your faculty contact.
- If courses or ECTS have changed, update the learning agreement. Complete the "Changes" section, have the host university validate it (minimum 30 ECTS), then send it for final validation.
- Free movers should check the SEMP (Swiss-European Mobility Programme) information on the free-mover page.

## On leaving the host university
- Obtain a certificate of attendance, completed, signed and stamped by the host university. Upload it as a PDF via the Mobility Portal.
- Complete the online mobility report, using the invitation sent to your student uni.lu email.
- Check that your bank details on the mobility contract are registered in the Student Guichet.
- Once all documents are submitted, the full mobility grant is paid within a minimum of 4 weeks.

## Upon returning
- Have the host university send the original transcript of grades directly to your faculty contact. An electronic version is accepted only if sent directly by the host university. Student-forwarded copies must be protected and certified.
- Keep a copy of all mobility documents.

## Key facts
- Steps: 1) Before leaving: valid European health insurance card, extra insurance and visa outside Europe, MAEE registration (Luxembourg students), International SOS app. 2) Within the month of arrival: send the signed learning agreement and update it if courses change. 3) On leaving: certificate of attendance uploaded via the Mobility Portal, online mobility report, bank details checked. 4) On return: original transcript sent by the host university to the faculty contact.
- Form needed: Learning agreement (and its Changes section); certificate of attendance; online mobility report.
- Office responsible: Outgoing Mobility Office, bri.outgoing@uni.lu; faculty contacts as listed.
- Deadline: Not published as dates. Learning agreement within the month of arrival; full grant paid a minimum of 4 weeks after all documents are submitted.
