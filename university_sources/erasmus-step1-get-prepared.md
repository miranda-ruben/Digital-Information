# Outgoing student mobility Erasmus+ - Step 1: Get prepared

## Timing
Prepare for your mobility semester one academic year before departure. Learn when and where you can study abroad and the prerequisites of each partner university for your programme.

## Mandatory info session (Bachelor students)
Attend the info session held at the Study Abroad Day, which takes place each year in November on the second Thursday. Master students may also attend.

## When (semester abroad)
- Bachelor students: usually semester 3, 4 or 5, depending on your programme.
  - Departure in semester 3 requires at least 15 ECTS in semester 1.
  - Departure in semester 4 requires at least 10 ECTS in semester 1.
- Master students: usually semester 3, to be verified for your programme.

## Where (destinations)
Erasmus+ spots are limited per academic year, study programme and receiving institution. The page refers to "the Erasmus+ publisher" for details but gives no link.

## Choosing a receiving institution
Check the partner institution's website for the pages on international exchanges under Erasmus+ and "Incoming students". Verify:
- Course catalogue: Bachelor students must choose courses totalling 30 ECTS.
- Teaching language: a minimum level of B2 is required. Some universities also ask for official tests such as TOEFL or IELTS.
- Semester availability: some partners accept exchange students only in winter or only in summer.
- Academic calendar: calendars can differ. For example, the French summer semester may start in early January, before the University of Luxembourg's exams end. Missing the start of classes is generally not acceptable.
- Campus location: courses may be held on campuses far from the main study location.
- Living costs: costs for food, accommodation and leisure vary by country and city. Northern European countries are generally more expensive than Southern ones.

## Key facts
- Steps: 1) Prepare one academic year before departure. 2) Attend the Study Abroad Day info session (mandatory for Bachelor students). 3) Check which semester you can go in and the requirements of each partner university (course catalogue, language B2, semester availability, calendar, campus, living costs).
- Form needed: None on this page.
- Office responsible: Outgoing Mobility Office (BRI), bri.outgoing@uni.lu (email from other sources).
- Deadline: No calendar date. Study Abroad Day: every year in November, on the second Thursday. ECTS conditions: at least 15 ECTS in semester 1 to leave in semester 3; at least 10 ECTS in semester 1 to leave in semester 4.
