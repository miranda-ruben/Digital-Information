OFFICIAL PAGE CONTENT — SUMMARY FOR UNIGUIDEAI

Overview:
The Erasmus+ programme is financed by the European Union. It allows students enrolled at the University of Luxembourg to spend a semester abroad at a partner institution in Europe, with recognition of credits obtained abroad.
The University of Luxembourg has participated in Erasmus since 2007. The page reports over 250 partner institutions in Europe and approximately 600 students using Erasmus+ for their mandatory mobility semester each academic year.
